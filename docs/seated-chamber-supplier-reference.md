# Seated chamber supplier reference

Checked 24 September 2026. This is a design and price reference for the shared-access baseline, not a purchase selection, Australian clinical approval or a quote delivered to Queensland.

## Real unit and photograph

- Product: OSAIL / OxySail YF-1003, single-person seated hard-shell chamber with a curved entry door and chair. [Manufacturer product page and photograph](https://oxysail.com/products/yf1003-hard-shell-hyperbaric-oxygen-chamber/).
- The website displays the manufacturer's own image from its website and links back to the product page. Image rights remain with the supplier. Written permission for lasting third-party publication is still worth obtaining; no copy is stored in this repository.
- The manufacturer's product page describes this model as intended for wellness and non-medical use. Its page also contains different maximum-pressure figures in prose and specifications, so the final configuration would need direct written confirmation.

## Published price basis

- [Jinan Yangfan's YF-1003 marketplace listing](https://0a157c49b0e4f57d.en.made-in-china.com/product/HTBpmKVMaiRL/China-High-Quality-Single-Person-Seated-Hyperbaric-Oxygen-Chamber-for-Nursing-Homes.html) lists US$20,000 for 1-4 units and US$19,000 for 10 or more units, FOB. The supplier account and product details should be checked directly before treating the OxySail and marketplace offers as one commercial quotation.
- The [Reserve Bank of Australia](https://www.rba.gov.au/statistics/frequency/exchange-rates.html) published US$0.7102 per A$1 on 23 September 2026, the latest published rate available during this check. Dividing the US-dollar listing by 0.7102 gives about A$28,200 for the one-unit US$20,000 figure and A$26,800 for the ten-unit US$19,000 figure. Currency movement and bank spreads will change a real payment.
- [FOB under Incoterms 2020](https://library.iccwbo.org/content/tfb/BOOKS/BK_0049/BK_0049_05_RulesSea.htm) refers to loading aboard a buyer-nominated vessel at a named port of shipment, not delivery to Australia. The listing does not name that port. Ocean freight, insurance, Australian port and inland transport, duty if applicable, import processing, GST, installation, servicing and Australian regulatory work need separate quotes. [Australian Border Force import costs](https://www.abf.gov.au/importing-exporting-and-manufacturing/importing/cost-of-importing-goods) explain the import side.
- The project's A$35,000 mass-produced baseline remains a draft target. The listing provides a promising price comparison, not evidence that an Australian landed, operational or clinical unit fits inside that target.

## Australian clinical pathway

The [Therapeutic Goods Administration](https://www.tga.gov.au/importing-supplying-medical-devices) says medical devices supplied in Australia generally need inclusion in the Australian Register of Therapeutic Goods unless an exemption applies. The linked YF-1003 has not been verified here as an Australian clinical device. A clinical co-operative version would have its own product, sponsor, documentation, operator training and intended-use review.
