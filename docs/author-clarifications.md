# Author clarifications

These notes record Luke Nathan Hayes' explanations of his own work where an automated or external reading missed the intended relationship.

## A Protopian Gambit

Clarified 30 August 2026.

The album holds an internal journey rather than a collection of unrelated themes:

- Song 1, *Primordial Consent 1,2,3, Infinity*, is about the divine self digital twin being born.
- *Kintsugi Protocol* is about repairing the self.
- *60 Days Set in Stone* is specifically the hyperbaric oxygen therapy song.

The website treats this birth, repair and embodied hyperbaric journey as a central narrative. Artistic meaning sits beside, rather than in place of, clinical evidence and personal professional care.

## Aura Geode research status

Clarified 24 September 2026.

Luke has declared the joined-up Aura Geode work as a research direction. The Geode trial itself is still a plan; it is not running and has no Geode study results. Published hyperbaric oxygen studies linked on the site are independent, completed work by other researchers. Planned Geode measures, participant groups, engineering tests and protocols are described as proposals until they are agreed and carried out.

The draft A$35,000 unit target is for a mass-produced baseline chamber using materials and manufacturing methods familiar in hyperbaric equipment, including Chinese-made products. It is not an Aura Geode price. Luke describes the Aura Geode as the sportscar model: a bespoke, higher-spec mineral-composite design using proposed high-purity quartz sand from Minjerribah with selected crystals and other materials. Its price is not yet set. Exploring claimed crystal resonance and etheric effects is a declared research direction, not a reported finding. Material records, physical tests, suitable comparisons and participant accounts are proposed ways to examine those questions.

## Aura Matrix Studio

Clarified 24 September 2026.

Aura Matrix Studio is an emergent, non-clinical public process for building a personal Aura of Intelligence through self-reflection and organising keywords and data for use with different artificial intelligence systems. It is separate from a future clinical Geode study.

## C-Hour and preventative wellbeing

Clarified 24 September 2026.

C-Hour is braided into the health co-operative idea. It encourages voluntary participation in food, movement, friendship, reflection, local resilience and other preventative wellbeing activities. Communities may shape reciprocal opportunities around shared equipment and experiences. A recorded hour has no monetary equivalent and does not price a health service; equipment, professional work and operating costs retain their own funding arrangements. Local access and reciprocity terms remain for the people involved to shape.
